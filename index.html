03 / Process

How the work gets made

From first treatment to final frame, we build films with a clear point of view and a production plan that can survive reality.

  1. 01

    Development

    We find the shape of the story before we find the schedule. Treatment, structure, tone references and a realistic production envelope.

  2. 02

    Pre-production

    Casting, locations, boards and a shot list that holds up on the day. Every decision is made while it is still cheap to change.

  3. 03

    Production

    Small crews, disciplined days, protected performance. We shoot for the edit, not for the footage count.

  4. 04

    Post & delivery

    Picture, sound, grade and masters in every format a broadcaster, platform or festival will ask for.
